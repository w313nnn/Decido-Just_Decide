# Decido

Decido is a campus-based decision-making platform that helps students decide what to eat or drink, from meals to desserts. By considering individual food preferences and customised budgets, Decido recommends nearby options to make everyday food decisions easier. Initially developed for UTAR Sungai Long, the project aims to expand to other university campuses in the future.

Inspired by the everyday question among friends: **"What should we eat today?"**
